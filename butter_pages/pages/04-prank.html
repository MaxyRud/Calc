The butter dish prank

It's on the table.
It's not butter.

A squeeze toy shaped like a stick of salted butter, real enough to fool anyone reaching for the toast. Lift the lid, then try to spread it.

Step 1 · lift the lid
Gotcha.It's a slow-rise squeeze toy. Give it a few seconds and it's ready to fool the next person.

How to pull it off

  1. Pick the momentBreakfast, a dinner party, a housemate who always steals your butter.
  2. Set the dishLabel facing out, lid on. It reads as a fresh 4 oz stick from across the table.
  3. Wait for the knifeIt dents instead of slicing. That is when they notice.
  4. Hand it overLet them squeeze it. It rises back on its own, ready for round two.

Good prank

  • Reveal it straight away
  • Keep it in a clean dish, away from real food
  • Wipe it with a damp cloth afterwards

Bad prank

  • Anywhere a small child could find it: it's not food and not for under 3s
  • Hidden in actual food, or warmed to look melted
  • Left in direct sun (it can deform)
