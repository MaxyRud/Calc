Care
Squeeze, press and roll. Don't fold it in half or stretch it to its limit: that's how squeeze toys tear. Keep it out of direct sun and away from heat. Wipe with a damp cloth and let it dry.
Safety
Not food: keep it away from anyone likely to bite it, and away from children under 3. Stop using it if it tears. Ask your supplier for EN 71 / ASTM F963 test reports before claiming "non-toxic".
