Observationsand care

  1. Pressing is fine. Folding it in half, or stretching it as far as it goes, is how TPR squeeze toys split. We did not run a fold test, and you shouldn't either.
  2. The surface is a little tacky and picks up lint. Rinse or wipe with a damp cloth and let it air-dry; it comes back clean.
  3. Heat softens TPR. Don't leave it on a sunny windowsill or in a hot car.
  4. It looks like food. Keep it away from anyone likely to bite it and from children under 3.
  5. No toxicity claims here. If you resell it, ask your supplier for EN 71 / ASTM F963 test reports.
