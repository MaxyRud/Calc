Ten ways to sell one very squishable can.

From two supplier photos of a soft-drink-can plush toy: the brand logo and wordmark removed, cut out, and tinted into four pastel colourways, then built into ten interactive layouts. Each one has its own type, palette and signature interaction: squish physics, a vending machine, a claw game, a fridge you can de-fog. They share one cart, so whatever you add in one layout stays in the next.

One cart, ten storefronts

The cart drawer, bundle maths (2 plushies −10%, 3 −15%), size upgrade and free-shipping bar are shared. Each layout themes the drawer to match its own look.

Logo-free photos

The supplier photos carry another company's logo and wordmark. Both were removed and the fabric rebuilt, so the pages sell the plush, not the brand. Only Arctic is the real photo; Blush, Glacier and Matcha are labelled previews.

Sample content

Prices, specs, ratings, reviews, codes and delivery dates are placeholders for the customer to replace. "Softdrink" and "Zero Can" are template names, not the supplier's brand.
