Ten ways to sell one shell-ejecting blaster.

From a single supplier photo of the 2011/M92 shell-ejecting soft blaster: cut out, logo removed, orange safety tip kept, recoloured into four colourways, then built into ten interactive layouts. Each one has its own type, palette and signature interaction. They share one cart, so whatever you add in one layout is still there in the next.

One cart, ten storefronts

The cart drawer, bundle maths (2 blasters −10%, 3 −15%) and free-shipping bar are shared. Each layout themes the drawer to match its own look.

Built from one photo

The supplied image was cut out from its background, the printed logo removed, and recoloured. Only Grape / Lime is the real photo; the other colourways are labelled as previews.

Sample content

Prices, specs, ratings, reviews and codes are placeholders for the customer to replace. Every page keeps the toy-safety copy: ages 14+, eye protection, orange tip on.
