Chapter I

An idea you can hear

Most toy blasters are about the thing that flies forward. The M92 is just as interested in the thing that flies sideways. Pull the slide back and a casing pops from the ejection port with a small, bright clack, then skitters across the floor. It is a tiny piece of theatre, and it turns out to be the part everyone remembers.

The rest of the package is sensibly done. The soft EVA bullets have rounded heads, there are no batteries to forget, and the scaled lime grip gives small hands something to hold on to. The orange tip stays on, as it should on any toy blaster.

In the box you get the blaster, six shells and twenty bullets. Shells are the part that goes missing under sofas, which is why the refill pack exists.

Every rack of the slide is a tiny piece of theatre, and the shell is the star.The Popshell Desk

Chapter III

The ritual, in three acts

i.

Load

Press a soft bullet into each shell, then slot the shells into the magazine.

ii.

Rack

Pull the slide all the way back and let it go. That's the clack.

iii.

Fire

Aim at a target, never a face. The bullet flies, the shell kicks out.
