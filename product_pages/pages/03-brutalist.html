04FAQ
How do the shells work?

Press a soft bullet into a shell, load the shells into the magazine, then rack the slide. When you fire, the bullet flies and the empty shell kicks out of the ejection port.

How far does it shoot?

Up to around 8 metres in our sample spec. Real-world distance depends on the bullet and how fully the slide is racked.

Is it safe for kids?

It's rated 14+ and should be used with adult supervision and eye protection. Never aim at faces or animals.

Will I lose the shells?

Probably a few. That's why there's a refill pack with 40 bullets and 12 shells.
