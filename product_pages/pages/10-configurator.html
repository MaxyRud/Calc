Questions

How do the shells work?

Press a soft bullet into each shell, load the shells into the magazine, rack the slide and fire. The empty shell ejects out of the port.

What age is it for?

Ages 14+ with adult supervision. Always wear eye protection.

How far does it shoot?

Up to around 8 metres (sample spec). It depends on the bullet and how fully the slide is racked.

Does it need batteries?

No. It's fully manual.

Can I buy more bullets and shells?

Yes, the refill pack adds 40 bullets and 12 shells.

Is the orange tip removable?

It must stay on. The orange tip marks it as a toy. Never paint or remove it.
