The idea

Each finger is a chain of three printed bones joined by M3 screws. A braided line (the tendon) is tied at the fingertip, runs through eyelets under every bone, through the palm and wrist, and winds onto a spool on a servo in the forearm.

When the servo turns, the spool reels the line in. The line sits 7.5 mm below each joint's pin, so the pull makes every joint bend at once and the finger curls. An elastic cord along the back pulls the finger open again when the servo turns back.
